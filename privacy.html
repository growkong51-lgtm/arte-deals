01 Scope & Who We Are

ARTE DEALS operates a vehicle marketplace serving the Philippines. This policy explains how we handle personal information and applies to everyone who uses the site, whether or not they hold an account.

For the purposes of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), ARTE DEALS acts as the Personal Information Controller for the data described here. We operate from Metro Manila, Philippines.

This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

03 Information We Do Not Collect

To be explicit, ARTE DEALS does not collect or store:

  • GCash MPINs, one-time PINs, or any payment credentials
  • Card numbers or bank account details
  • Government identification documents
  • Continuous or background location tracking
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking identifiers
  • Sensitive personal information as defined by the Data Privacy Act, including race, health, religion, or political affiliation

07 Where Data Is Stored

Our database is hosted in Singapore. Images, email delivery, and website hosting may involve servers in other countries operated by the providers listed above.

Where personal information is transferred outside the Philippines, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected to a standard consistent with the Data Privacy Act, including relying on providers that publish their own data protection commitments.

08 Payments

Subscription payments are made through GCash, outside our systems. We do not operate a payment gateway and never receive your GCash credentials.

What we receive is the reference number you submit, which we match against payments received in order to activate your subscription. We retain these references as proof of payment.

09 Location Data

9.1 Vehicle location

When posting, sellers choose a place name from a search list. The chosen area and its approximate coordinates are stored with the listing and shown publicly, so buyers can filter by location.

9.2 "Nearest to me"

If a buyer sorts by distance, their browser asks permission and the calculation happens on their own device. Their position is not transmitted to us and is not stored. Declining the request simply returns the listings in their normal order.

10 Messages & Moderation

Messages are stored so both participants retain their conversation. They are private between those two people.

Administrators may access messages where necessary to investigate a report, a suspected breach of our terms, or a suspected fraud, and where we are required to do so by law or lawful request. We do not read messages routinely.

Never share these

Do not send passwords, verification codes, GCash PINs, card details, or photographs of identity documents through the Platform. No one from ARTE DEALS will ever ask for them.

12 Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed — know that your information is being processed, and why.
  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectify — correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase or block — have your information removed, subject to legal retention.
  • Object — object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — receive your information in a usable electronic format.
  • Damages — seek compensation for harm caused by inaccurate or unlawfully used information.

To exercise any of these, email artedeals1@gmail.com from the address on your account. We aim to respond within fifteen (15) days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

13 Deleting Your Account

Email us from your registered address asking for deletion. On receipt we will:

  • Remove your listings and photographs from the site
  • Delete your profile, including your name and photograph
  • Remove your account and sign-in credentials

Some information may be retained where the law requires it or where we need it to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud — for example, payment records and reports made against the account. Messages you sent may remain visible to the person who received them, as those records also belong to them.

14 Security

We apply organisational and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the data:

  • Passwords are stored using one-way encryption and are never visible to us.
  • The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic is encrypted in transit.
  • Email verification is required before an account can be used.
  • Database rules enforce, at the data layer, that one account cannot read another's private information.
  • Administrative access is restricted to a separate, dedicated account.
  • Payment details are held in the database, visible only to signed-in users, rather than in public files.

No system is entirely secure. Use a password unique to this site, and never share a verification code with anyone.

15 Data Breach

If a breach occurs that is likely to give rise to a real risk of serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the affected users within the period required by law, describing what happened, what information was involved, and what steps to take.

16 Stored on Your Device

We store a sign-in token in your browser so you remain signed in between visits. It is essential to the site working and is not used for advertising or tracking.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or analytics profiling. Clearing your browser data will sign you out.

17 Children

ARTE DEALS is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it and delete the associated information.

18 Changes

We may update this policy as the site develops or the law changes. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be announced on the Platform, and continued use afterwards indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
