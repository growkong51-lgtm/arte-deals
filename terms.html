08 ARTE DEALS PRO

PRO is an optional paid subscription for Sellers. It includes:

  • Six active Listings per Cycle instead of three
  • A PRO badge displayed on the Seller's profile and Listings
  • Contact links (Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp) shown to Buyers on the Seller's profile
  • Increased likelihood of appearing higher in browse results

8.1 Term

A PRO subscription runs for one month from the date of activation.

8.2 No automatic renewal

PRO does not renew automatically and no recurring charge is ever made. When the month ends the account reverts to Standard. To continue, the Seller makes a new payment and submits a new reference number.

8.3 Effect of expiry

When PRO expires, the Seller's allowance returns to three Listings per Cycle. Listings already posted are not deleted, but the Seller cannot post beyond the Standard limit until the next Cycle or until PRO is renewed.

10 Placement & Visibility

Browse results are ordered by a weighted shuffle that changes on each visit, so that Listings are not permanently ranked by age. Increased visibility is not a guarantee of views, enquiries, or a sale. Buyers may also sort results by newest, price, or distance, which disregards weighting entirely.
